Terms and Conditions
Data Privacy and Security

By creating an account with EmotionTrack, you acknowledge and agree to the following terms regarding your personal information and data:

1. Data Collection and Use
  • All personal information and wellness data you provide will be kept strictly confidential
  • Your data will only be accessible to authorized guidance office personnel
  • Information will be used solely for counseling, support, and mental wellness purposes
  • We collect emotional tracking data, assessment results, and consultation messages to provide personalized support
2. Data Security
  • All data is encrypted and stored securely using industry-standard security measures
  • Access to your information is restricted to authorized counselors and administrators only
  • We implement robust security protocols to protect your sensitive information
  • Your login credentials are protected using secure password hashing
3. Professional Use
  • Your wellness data may be reviewed by qualified mental health professionals
  • Information will be used to identify students who may need additional support
  • Assessment results help counselors provide appropriate interventions
  • All interactions follow professional counseling and ethical guidelines
4. Confidentiality
  • Your personal information will not be shared with unauthorized third parties
  • Parents/guardians may be contacted only in cases of serious safety concerns
  • Aggregated, anonymous data may be used for research and program improvement
  • Your identity will always be protected in any research or statistical use
5. Your Rights
  • You have the right to access your personal data at any time
  • You can request corrections to any inaccurate information
  • You may withdraw from the platform at any time
  • You can contact the guidance office with any privacy concerns
Remember: EmotionTrack is a supportive tool designed to help you track and improve your mental wellness. If you're experiencing a mental health emergency, please contact emergency services or speak directly with a counselor immediately.
